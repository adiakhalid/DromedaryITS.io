Product and Trigonometric Rules

Example 1: \(y = \sin(x)\cdot \cos(x)\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &= \frac{d}{dx}(\sin(x)\cos(x)) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \cos(x)\cdot\frac{d}{dx}(\sin(x)) + \sin(x)\cdot\frac{d}{dx}(\cos(x)) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \cos(x)\cdot \cos(x) + \sin(x)\cdot (-\sin(x)) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \cos^2(x) - \sin^2(x) \end{aligned} $$

Example 2: \(y = x^2\sin^{-1}(x)\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sin^{-1}(x)\cdot \frac{d}{dx}(x^2) + x^2\cdot \frac{d}{dx}(\sin^{-1}(x)) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sin^{-1}(x)\cdot 2x + x^2\cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= 2x\sin^{-1}(x) + \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \end{aligned} $$

Example 3: \(y = \tan(x)\cdot \sec(x) + \sin^2(x)\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sec(x)\cdot \frac{d}{dx}(\tan(x)) + \tan(x)\cdot \frac{d}{dx}(\sec(x)) + \frac{d}{dx}(\sin^2(x)) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sec(x)\cdot \sec^2(x) + \tan(x)\cdot (\sec(x)\tan(x)) + 2\sin(x)\cos(x) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sec^3(x) + \tan^2(x)\sec(x) + 2\sin(x)\cos(x) \\ \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} &= \sec(x)(\sec^2(x) + \tan^2(x)) + 2\sin(x)\cos(x) \end{aligned} $$
