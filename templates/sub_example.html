Arithmetic: Subtraction Examples

Example 1: \(15 - 3\)

Solution: 

      15
    -  3
    -----
      12

Example 2: \(76 - 24\)

Solution: 

      76
    - 24
    -----
      52

Example 3: \(83 - 45\)

Solution: 

borrow → 1 from the tens digit 8
