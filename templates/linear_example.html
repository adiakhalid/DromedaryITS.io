Algebra: Linear Equation Examples

Example 1: \(95x - 12 = 23x + 9\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Move terms with } x \text{ to one side and constants on the other side:} \\ 95x - 23x &= 9 + 12 \\ 72x &= 21 \\ \text{Divide both sides by 72:} \\ x &= \frac{21}{72} \\ \text{Simplify fraction:} \\ x &= \frac{7}{24} \end{aligned} $$

Example 2: \(9x + 8 + 16x = 22\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Move terms with } x \text{ to one side and constants on the other side:} \\ 9x + 16x &= 22 - 8 \\ 25x &= 15 \\ \text{Divide both sides by 25:} \\ x &= \frac{15}{25} \\ \text{Simplify fraction:} \\ x &= \frac{3}{5} \end{aligned} $$

Example 3: \(11x + 1 = 10x - 9\)

Solution:

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{Move terms with } x \text{ to one side and constants on the other side:} \\ 11x - 10x &= -9 - 1 \\ x &= -10 \end{aligned} $$
